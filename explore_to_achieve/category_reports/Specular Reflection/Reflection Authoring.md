## Operation and native predicate

`mirror_survey_round_trip` requests an initial stop, a side inspection stop, and return to the measured starting station. The side stop requires at least 0.25 m XY displacement and at least 10 degrees of bearing change around the **measured native mirror**. Return requires at most 0.12 m XY error. Each ordered stop requires six consecutive control samples, heading within 20 degrees of the mirror, native linear speed at most 0.02 m/s and angular speed at most 0.1 rad/s.

Qualification reads the actual original mirror, prop and named floor, checks source optical poses and proposed initialization, native floor contacts, room membership, uprightness and conservative visual-asset clearance. During execution, the evaluator permanently rejects any sampled nonfloor robot contact, lost floor support, room departure, optical asset movement or displacement exceeding the existing controller's per-step movement bound. Every success requires the full ordered route and current terminal stop. Repeated observation recording cannot advance stages; stationary motion and QA cannot pass. Hooks read native state only and write private evidence; they never step the simulator, move assets or issue robot actions.

Optical preservation tolerances are 0.02 m against source construction poses at qualification, and 0.01 m against measured episode-start poses during execution, with one degree orientation tolerance. A 0.03 m conservative separation from the native optical asset AABBs supplements normal robot collision guards because those assets remain visual-only. These bounds are explicit proposals, not measured reachability guarantees. Native reflected-pixel coverage, recognition and exact camera equivalence are not certified by this navigation predicate.
