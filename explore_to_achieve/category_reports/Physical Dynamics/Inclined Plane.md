## Final implementation and independent physical predicate

Converter: [physical_dynamics_inclined_plane.py](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/core/esi_converters/physical_dynamics_inclined_plane.py), version `1.2.0`. `inspect`, `convert`, `convert_all` and stratified `sample` use the shared `ConverterBase`, `original_scene` and `physical_task` contracts. Native hooks use the owned `category_operation` API. Qualification and evaluation only read native state; the module contains no robot controls, object setters, simulation stepping or legacy placement calls.

The public operation asks the robot to grasp the original object at its saved start, lift it clear of its support, carry it to the original slope, establish upper-face support, open the gripper, withdraw and observe without touching for at least one simulated second. The original question remains auxiliary. Stable, sliding and falling outcomes are all valid completed physical trials.

Exact scoring requirements:

1. Native qualification checks the original bindings, target movability/collidability, stationary initial support by the original floor or measured slope top, saved target root within 0.05 m, original slope pose/scale, source floor height, measured mesh friction, target contact reporting, empty gripper and actual robot floor support/room/collision state. Qualification does not certify reach or manipulation.
2. Native gripper readback must show the target held with an active constraint, and its AABB minimum Z must rise at least 0.05 m from its measured episode start.
3. Before detachment, target contact must lie on the original slope upper face: contact local Z within 0.01 m of the top, contact XY within the source rectangle plus 0.005 m, absolute local normal Z at least 0.5, AABB centre projected inside the slope footprint and above its top, and no floor contact.
4. An open, empty gripper with no active constraint or pending release must leave the object supported on the slope, without robot/foreign-object contact, within 0.5 s of detachment. The first such sample starts the release trace.
5. Require at least 1.0 s and 20 distinct native samples with no gap above 0.1 s. Duplicate reads add no duration, reversed native time invalidates the episode, and robot/foreign-object interference invalidates the trace. The original slope and target physics must remain valid, and the target must still be unheld at terminal measurement. Regrasp clears the release trace. No wrong-object grasp is allowed.

Native simulator time, physics dt and simulator-step dt are recorded rather than inferred from frame count or wall time. The evaluator separately reports maximum XY displacement, native support/contact, velocities and a released-stability measure. The legacy fall-height screen is explicitly named: `AABB_min_z < measured_floor_top + 0.05 m`; a low object can satisfy this screen while still on the ramp. Stability uses maximum trace drift at most 0.03 m and stationary supported samples spanning at least 0.45 s within the final 0.5 s, with linear speed at most 0.02 m/s and angular speed at most 0.1 rad/s. These derived outcomes do not read source answers or source success flags.
