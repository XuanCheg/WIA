## Conversion and terminal predicates

The converter uses original_scene / physical_task and inherits the shared per-source diagnostic and candidate-revision machinery. Inspect validates the three original assets and poses, camera records, floor identity, public object order and nondegenerate triangle. Neither source success flags nor the original triangle answer participate in physical success.

Two deterministic recipes reuse the formation:

- `triangle_circuit_return`: prefer this when a complete circle at radius 0.9, 1.1, 1.3 or 1.5 m passes the original room-map screen. Native signed angle increments must total at least one full turn about **each** of the three initial native vertices, all in the same direction. The robot must travel at least 2 m, make a 0.5 m excursion, return within 0.25 m and stop for 0.5 native seconds.
- `ordered_vertex_survey_return`: use when there is a screened start but no complete circular map path. Approach each original vertex in the public question order from three distinct inspection positions. Robot-to-vertex root distance is 0.65–1.5 m, the measured robot front faces the inspected vertex within 30 degrees, and every pair of inspection positions is at least 0.4 m apart. Travel at least 1 m, make a 0.5 m excursion, return within 0.25 m and stop for 0.5 native seconds. A bounded static search must supply three possible positions; their native connectivity and visibility still require qualification/execution. Private evaluation saves each actual visit's position, time, distance and heading.

Both recipes require continuous measured base samples (at most 0.10 m displacement and 0.10 s native-clock gap), upright actual contact with the bound source floor and no other external robot contact, at least 0.35 m clearance from every native object AABB footprint, and a base position within 2.5 m of the initial formation centre. Every original vertex must retain its initial native position and AABB within 0.02 m and orientation within 5 degrees throughout. Repeated `record()` calls accumulate no route, winding, stage or stationary duration. Physical callbacks use measured simulator time, not assumed callback frequency.

Sampling follows existing controller env-step callbacks; no continuous 120 Hz contact trace or native execution is claimed. A measured base heading is an operational orientation condition, not proof that an object was recognized or visible.

Native qualification independently requires the correct bound collidable floor, source-root positions within 0.035 m, source orientations within 5 degrees, object bottoms within 0.035 m of the native floor top, and actual supported upright R1 contact on that floor. Private pose, floor, geometry and trajectory evidence stays in private evaluation output. Public content contains only source question/object descriptions, operational instructions and fixed success thresholds.
