## Retained implementation

Converter version: **1.2.0**. Owned implementation: [spatial_relations_physical_contact.py](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/core/esi_converters/spatial_relations_physical_contact.py).

`inspect`, `convert` and `convert_all` implement deterministic whole-category conversion using `ConverterBase`, `original_scene`, `physical_task` and the original pure asset constructor. The two recipes are `separate_then_contact` (default) and `contact_then_separate`. Both require two real grasp/lift/release phases. The smaller saved AABB volume determines the manipulation target with deterministic tie breaking; it is an effort heuristic, not measured graspability. Its original partner remains the anchor.

The independent private evaluator requires native holding, target identity and an active grasp constraint, followed by at least 0.05 m lift of the target's lowest point. Each phase then requires a completely released, empty, open gripper; native `Touching` for contact, or `Touching=false` plus a conservative measured AABB separation of at least 0.05 m for separation. Both objects must touch and be `OnTop` of the original floor, remain collidable/movable, stay in the source room and within 1.5 m of the original pair midpoint, and have linear/angular speeds at most 0.02 m/s and 0.1 rad/s. The anchor must remain within 0.03 m and 15 degrees of its initial measured pose. A wrong grasp, observed anchor disturbance or unavailable measurement prevents success. The second phase requires fresh held-lift evidence; a valid initial relation cannot complete either phase.

Stability means **15 consecutive native measurements following control steps**, through the existing controller `on_step` callback. The installed original R1 template uses 30 Hz control and 120 Hz physics, so this is approximately 0.5 s of sampled stability under that template, not 15 raw physics substeps and not proof of unobserved intermediate states. Repeated evidence reads never increase the stability counter. Losing the final relation, support, release or other terminal criterion revokes success. Native qualifier/evaluator hooks only read simulator state; all robot actions remain in the existing guarded controller.

Every adapted variant declares one initialization-only R1 stance, screened on original 1 cm traversability/room maps: at least 0.42 m map clearance, 0.65 m from each saved pair AABB and at most 2.5 m from its midpoint, selecting the pixel nearest the first source observer. Native source-floor wheel contacts, body room membership and absence of other external contacts remain mandatory. Map evidence is private and does not prove navigation or reach. Object physics is explicitly enabled before construction and after original postprocessing, without fixed-base changes, asset replacement, scaling or target relocation. The current builder resets poses but does not reset `visual_only`; the second physics phase also guards against source postprocessing resets. Every scene/task retains the empty `original` variant. No omitted source mechanisms or robot-free builder were found, so category initialization and R1 injection are unnecessary for this category.

The original source question, answers, options and metadata remain unchanged and preserved in private QA records. Public operation text names the actually loaded categories, describes the physical goal and never includes correct answers, object/model identifiers, coordinates, maps, reference-camera poses or native scorer state. All generated physical tasks remain `pending`; the existing loader rejects their execution until qualification/review requirements are met.
