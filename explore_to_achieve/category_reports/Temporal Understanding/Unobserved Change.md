## Retained implementation and exact predicates

The owned [converter](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/core/esi_converters/temporal_understanding_unobserved_change.py) implements `inspect`, `convert`, and deterministic `convert_all` on the existing shared API. Version: `unobserved_change_v1.2`. It uses `original_scene`, `physical_task`, the owned `category_initialization` hooks and the read-only `category_operation` adapter. No shared code, original JSON, approved records, other category reports or root research log were edited.

`original` remains an empty patch variant. `adapted` defaults to phase 2; `saved_image1` and `saved_image2` restore the corresponding source snapshots only at initialization. The original CPU replay parser and preload configuration helper supply exact source asset identities, fixed container roots, identity orientations and offstage inactive content. Active contents start at the source replay's saved AABB center. `object_physics` makes active contents movable and enables container/content collisions before construction and again after source postprocessing. Original R1 configuration and scene physics are retained; the current generic active builder already supplies R1, so no duplicate robot is added. Source observer/viewer cameras are retained.

Three operation recipes are available through `ConversionContext.config['unobserved_operation']`; `unobserved_initial_phase` accepts `image1` or `image2`:

- `phase_inventory_return` (default): choose an item present in the initialized snapshot, physically open its original lid if needed, grasp and extract it, then return and release it in the same box.
- `phase_inventory_extract`: hold a selected item completely above its original box after extraction.
- `box_inspection_route`: physically inspect a box exterior from two distinct views and return to the start. This is also the automatic operation when the chosen phase contains no items.

Extraction requires the measured native grasp identity, `holding=True`, an active native grasp constraint, a target AABB-bottom rise of at least 0.05 m relative to its measured episode initial state, native `Inside=False`, and clearance of at least 0.01 m above the measured closed initial container rim. Return requires a prior measured held extraction followed by an empty open gripper, native `Inside=True`, native `Touching` with the original container, linear speed at most 0.02 m/s, angular speed at most 0.1 rad/s, position within 0.04 m and orientation within 10 degrees of the measured initial placement. Other contents must remain in their original boxes and near their initial placements.

An inspection route requires two native robot positions 0.7–1.8 m from the same measured box, at least 0.65 m and 60 degrees apart, each at least 0.5 m from the initial position, and an enabled robot RGB view containing at least 64 native box pixels with minimum extent 8 pixels. Private segmentation is enabled only during initialization. Its RGB must match the actual delivered Agent RGB; original/Review/reference images do not count. The robot must return within 0.2 m of its measured initial XY with an empty open gripper and no obstacle contact. This exterior route does not establish visibility of closed-box interiors or temporal change recognition.

All terminal goals require 15 consecutive controller physics samples, a nonempty guarded action history, valid measured initial state and preserved source identities/root poses/native scales. Repeated `record()` calls cannot create stability samples. Qualification and evaluators have no scene-state setters, simulator stepping or robot action calls. Original QA, rendering success flags and source answers do not establish operation success.

### Actual operations, predicates and initial QA boundaries

The three recipes are useful and distinguish real terminal behavior: held extraction checks acquisition and clearance; extraction/return adds release, containment, contact and settling; exterior inspection provides navigation/perception work when the selected snapshot is empty. No saved pose, source answer or render success flag directly proves success. Their practical reach, lid access and controller behavior remain unmeasured.

The implemented extraction predicate requires the correct native held identity, holding and active grasp constraint, at least 5 cm rise, native `Inside=False` and 1 cm clearance above the initial closed container rim. Return requires prior measured extraction, an empty open gripper, native `Inside=True`, container contact, low linear/angular speed and return within 4 cm/10 degrees of the measured episode start. Other contents are protected. Inspection requires two delivered-RGB-matched visible-box visits at the declared separated native positions, then return near the initial robot position. Initial validity, source identities/container roots/scales, action-step progression and 15 controller sampling callbacks gate success.
