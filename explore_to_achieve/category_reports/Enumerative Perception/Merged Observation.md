## Operation recipes and native predicates

The default `retrieve_return` recipe requires observing the compact cluster from multiple robot views, retaining an initial auxiliary count, and physically grasping, lifting and returning one accessible original target. `inventory_return` repeats the operation for every distinct original target, using native identity sets to prevent duplicate credit. Both recipes are generated through `physical_task` and the owned `category_operation` adapter. The [alternate complete manifest](/home/zhouxinyang/esi_category_conversion/20261014_v2/categories/831f2176ff10b7c4adb1/operation_variants/inventory_return/batch_manifest.json) contains all 60 sources; the default manifest is the automatic Review input.

Qualification reads actual native pose, quaternion, AABB, velocities, physics flags, `Touching`, `OnTop`, R1 chassis room segmentation and contact lists. Every target must be movable/collidable and stationary, retain its source dimensions/layout within declared tolerances, and connect to the original floor through a native support chain. This admits the original stacks; an upper target is not incorrectly required to touch the floor directly. R1 must stand in the original room with floor contact and no obstacle contact. Missing state measurements fail closed. Qualification does not certify grasp reach or task execution.

Physical success requires an active native grasp constraint and affirmative native holding state, with the target's lowest AABB point at least 0.05 m above its measured episode baseline. A target is credited as returned only after open, empty, fully released gripper state and measured placement/support near its baseline. Delayed settling after release is permitted. The final cluster must restore individual positions within 0.03 m, orientations within 15 degrees, pairwise displacement vectors within 0.03 m, and all initial floor/target support edges. All targets must stay below 0.02 m/s linear and 0.1 rad/s angular speed for 15 advancing controller/physics-state samples. Repeated record calls cannot increase stability. A wrong-object grasp or failed initial qualification prevents success. Full inventory requires complete distinct identity coverage; source answers and saved success flags never determine terminal success.

The hooks contain no simulation steps, robot commands or state setters. Actions remain in the existing guarded R1 base, arm, trunk and gripper controls. Evaluator state and trajectories are private.
