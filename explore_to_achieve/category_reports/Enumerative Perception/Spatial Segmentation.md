## Implemented operation and private evidence

The owned [converter](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/core/esi_converters/enumerative_perception_spatial_segmentation.py) implements `inspect`, `convert`, `convert_all`, stratified `sample`, `qualify_operation` and `create_evaluator`. Final version: `spatial_segmentation_v1.1`.

Two deterministic recipes are available through `spatial_segmentation_operation` in conversion configuration; without an override, SHA-256 of seed and source identity selects the recipe:

- `segmented_access_patrol_return`: approach every original occupied chair region separately, pause facing it, then return and park at departure.
- `segmented_two_approach_patrol_return`: perform the same patrol with two approach positions at least 0.50 m apart for each region before returning.

Both recipes use existing guarded base translation/rotation controls. No converter, qualification hook or evaluator issues robot actions, changes object states, moves assets, or obtains an Agent observation from review images. The physical objective has no fixed answer count or private object names. The initial source question remains public auxiliary context; its answer, metadata, source poses, camera records, bindings and evaluator configuration remain private.

Exact independent physical predicate: after an actual departure excursion of at least 0.50 m and at least 1.0 m of sampled real travel, acquire a stable approach witness for every bound target group. Each witness requires 0.25 s of advancing native simulation time, speed at most 0.05 m/s, angular speed at most 0.10 rad/s, target-centre standoff in [0.55, 1.25] m, base heading error at most 30 degrees, chassis clearance of at least 0.05 m from every task chair, native wheel-floor support and no non-floor robot contacts. First witnesses for different regions must be at least 0.35 m apart; the two-approach recipe additionally requires a 0.50 m separation from that group's first witness. Finish within 0.30 m of the measured departure position, in its original native room label, stopped for 0.25 s. Targets and chairs must preserve episode-start position within 0.02 m, orientation within 5 degrees and AABB dimensions within 0.02 m throughout. Native trajectory increments must respect the actual guarded controller's translation and rotation speed/tolerance bounds. Same-clock rereads cannot create dwell evidence, and discontinuities, unsampled terminal motion, unavailable measurements or unsafe states permanently fail the episode.

Qualification separately reads actual bindings, source-vs-native bounds/poses, the original under-chair grouping, collidable chair anchors, unchanged visual-only target physics, native start room, chassis clearance, wheel-floor rays, floor contacts and guarded-control capabilities. This is initialization qualification, not a route feasibility certificate. The predicate never consumes source render `success`, historical visibility labels, the count answer or a mock operation as physical evidence. Access and heading do not establish pixel visibility or recognition.
