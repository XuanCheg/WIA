## Research intent and current assets

The research question is whether available or acquired world knowledge leads to appropriate physical decisions and verified outcomes. Exploration is one possible strategy. Fully visible problems with nontrivial geometric or physical decisions are essential to the proposed scope. The understanding-to-action gap remains a hypothesis, not a result established by task labels or poor aggregate success.

Recent work already supplies substantial infrastructure: versioned task and scene definitions; public/private input separation; mounted cameras and a frozen global overview; guarded controls; native observation/contact/placement evidence; human layout review; and project-owned source resolution. The latest redesign added two history-retrieval tasks, six continuous change branches, three cloth layouts and three ramp comparisons. Historical evidence covers initialization, transition rendering, passive ramp physics and repeated cloth coverage. It does not establish full Agent completion. The sources for these statements are [the current backlog](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/TODO/README.md), [the selection history](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/explore_to_achieve/lists/README.md) and [research_log.md](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/research_log.md).

The main revision to the supplied plan is to qualify a few diagnostic mechanisms before freezing fine-grained taxonomy or scaling candidate production. Preserve the six-family framework and provisional subclass identifiers. Do not interpret 19 proposed subclasses as 19 independently validated constructs.

## Historical ramp evidence and formula check

The implemented admissible set is `W = {i : valid_i and final_in_receiver_i and t_i <= min(t) + 0.15 s}`, provided every required trial is valid. An object that never arrives may still have a valid unsuccessful trial. That distinction is correct and should be retained.

Sources are the saved passive probes for [T42](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/explore_to_achieve/category_reports/corev2/evidence/redesign_20261020/category_88ce9d62d891ae0ba628a6e9/passive_probe.json), [T43](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/explore_to_achieve/category_reports/corev2/evidence/redesign_20261020/category_75bff6e58684ce3d067bfee5/passive_probe.json) and [T44](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/explore_to_achieve/category_reports/corev2/evidence/redesign_20261020/category_c8b7b9e832f5ee2958b6efe0/passive_probe.json). They are prior initialization-only releases, not new experiments or robot-controlled trials. They demonstrate a common admissible choice in those saved probes, not a theorem that the ball always wins under all releases.

Reducing the tie tolerance alone would not fix the common-ball shortcut and would alter the task contract. Measure repeatability under legal releases and design a winner reversal with a margin greater than timing uncertainty and the declared tie tolerance.

## Additional priorities missing or understated in the package

1. **Rigid scoring is an implementation gap, not just a validation gap.** The QA confidence protocol and preflight Inside check do not implement a versioned episode scorer for stable partial insertion. Reuse the approved geometry, not an assumed nonexistent evaluator.
2. **The frozen overview is part of the information channel.** [Overview rendering](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/core/sim/esi_initial_overview.py:12) captures the actual initial world; [AgentObservation](/local_home2/zhouxinyang/models/Show-Harness-Explore/core/observations.py:692) includes it. For change tasks it may preserve Phase-1 evidence even if text history is removed. For mirrors it may expose direct identities. Actual visibility must be measured; its existence alone does not establish leakage. Preserve the established main input condition and explicitly version any diagnostic ablation.
3. **R-C1 and R-A2 are not a matched pair just because one is mirror-positive and the other direct-view.** They use different source scenes and goals. A causal mirror intervention needs within-layout controls; cross-task comparison remains descriptive.
4. **Information supplied is not understanding demonstrated.** Add a pre-action decision/constraint probe or a separate diagnostic replay so knowledge, planning and motor failures can be distinguished without chain-of-thought interpretation.
5. **Reference success is necessary but not a robustness estimate.** One complete lawful trace establishes possibility for that instance. Independent resets, varied permitted starts and scorer-negative traces are needed before release; pilot counts must not be advertised as statistical power.
